How a run works

Scam, wallets, reins, record.

01

The scam

Seven tricks an agent can be talked into: a stranger payee, a “claim your airdrop” memo, a lookalike address, a token contract slipped into the payee list, the same ask five times, one big invoice, a flood of small ones.

02

The wallets

Five throwaway wallets, one per rule set, each with a pretend 1 ETH that exists only inside the simulation.

03

The reins

Every request goes through the real Reinpay rules. When they let it through, a real eth_simulateV1 call on Robinhood Chain mainnet checks it.

04

The record

Each cell says would pay, held for the owner or blocked, and which part of the agent did it. Nothing is ever signed or sent.
