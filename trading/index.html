BOT RULES · SAME ENGINE AS REINPAY TRADING

Decisions run on each market's hourly close, exactly like the Trading Desk: momentum buys when the last hour closed up by the signal, buy the dip buys when price is that far under the 24 hour high. The Keeper checks take profit and stop loss on every live quote, every few seconds. 0.2% of each real trade goes to the public buyback and burn wallet. Trading can lose money; paper results are not a promise of anything.
