STRATEGY AND RULESCHANGE AND REPLAY

Candles are hourly prices of each market's deepest Robinhood Chain pool (GeckoTerminal, from on-chain swaps); the live price is a KyberSwap quote in your browser every 15 seconds. Each paper trade pays half the measured round trip cost of a 1,000 USDG swap on each side, plus Reinpay's 0.2%. Price impact is estimated from a 10,000 USDG quote. Live trades are simulated with eth_simulateV1 on mainnet before they count. A replay is not a promise of anything.

MAINNET MODE · REAL TRADES · YOUR WALLET SIGNSNOT CONNECTED

Real trades use your own USDG on Robinhood Chain mainnet through the KyberSwap route. Each one is quoted, checked against your caps and simulated on mainnet from your wallet; your wallet only asks you to sign after you press Yes. Approvals are exact, never unlimited. 0.2% of each trade goes to the public buyback and burn wallet 0xc651…9317. Trading can lose money.
