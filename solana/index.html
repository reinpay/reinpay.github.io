BOT RULES

Buys a trending token when it is up by the signal in the last hour (but not more than the max), the last 5 minutes are not falling, buys beat sells, and it passes the rug check: mint and freeze authority revoked, no Token-2022 transfer tax, enough liquidity, an old enough pool, and a buy-then-sell quote that costs less than half the stop loss. The Keeper values every position by a real Jupiter sell quote and sells on take profit, stop loss, trailing stop or max hold. Empty token accounts are closed so their rent comes back.
