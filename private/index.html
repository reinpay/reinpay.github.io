Pay privately. Keep the reins.

A design for agent payments on Robinhood Chain that do not put your wallet on display, still pass every Reinpay rule, and can be proven clean when someone asks. In development: nothing on this page is live, and it takes no deposits.

Three steps

Shield, pay, prove.

The same reins as every Reinpay payment, with one difference: the payee sees the payment, not your whole wallet.

01

Shield

Move USDG from your wallet into a private balance. On chain, the pool sees a deposit, not what you do with it later.

02

Pay inside your rules

The agent asks as always. Payee list, budgets and your approval line run first; only then is a private payment made.

03

Prove it is clean

Show that your deposits are in a set that leaves out blocked addresses, without showing which deposits are yours.

04

Keep the receipt

Every private payment still ends in a receipt on your own computer, so you can replay why it was allowed.

Straight answers

What it will and will not do.

StatusA design in development. There is no pool, no contract and no deposit address. Anything claiming to be a Reinpay private pool today is not us.
Your rules still applyPayee list, budgets, the approval line and freeze run before any private payment, exactly as they do now.
Clean by designIt is meant for keeping ordinary payments private, not for hiding where money came from. The clean proof leaves out blocked addresses.
Before anything is liveThe contracts and the proofs will be published, verified on Sourcify and reviewed before Reinpay lets an agent use them.
